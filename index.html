WHO WE ARE ✦

Five people.
One sound.

The Platinum Marimba Band is a group of five 15-year-old Curro Somerset West learners who share a love for music, performance and, obviously, marimbas.

Over time, playing together has become about much more than getting the notes right. It has given us a place to laugh, create, challenge ourselves and make memories together. Every rehearsal, competition and performance has brought us closer as a band.

Our goal is to broaden our reach and share our love for music with more people. We want to perform at new venues, connect with our community and inspire other young musicians to discover just how much joy music can bring.

THE GIRLS BEHIND THE MALLETS ♡

Meet the Platinums.

Five very different personalities. One very big love for music.

PLATINUM 01

Ayla Daniel-Ramouthar

I love playing marimba because of the people I share the music with. Performing together creates a sense of synchronicity that connects us on a deeper level. The sound of the African marimba is something I personally treasure as it carries a warmth that feels grounding, almost like it connects you to something bigger than yourself. I feel a certain energy flowing through us when we play together, and it always lifts one’s mood.

PLATINUM 02

Kiara Lovett

I love playing marimba because it’s really fun and different from other instruments. I like how everyone has to work together to make the music sound good, and it’s always satisfying when we finally get a song right. I also love the sound of the marimba and how energetic the music can be. It’s something I genuinely enjoy doing, especially when I’m playing with other people.

PLATINUM 03

Mia van Tubbergh

I love marimba and music because it allows me to express myself while having fun with others. I love the energy and unique sound of the marimba, and how everyone has to work together to create something amazing. There’s nothing better than finally getting a song right after practising together.

PLATINUM 04

Olivia Bain

I love marimba because it really brings our band together in a strong bond. The musicality, rhythm and songs we share and love together is truly special to me. Marimba is our escape from the stress at school, home and just anything — marimba always makes us smile at the end of the day.

PLATINUM 05

Sitara Pillay

I love marimba because, although I have experienced different instruments, genres and ensembles, there is something about marimba that feels completely different. It is more than just an instrument; it is an entire experience. Creating something together with people you care about is incredibly special. Platinum Marimba has given me friendships, memories and a sense of belonging that I will carry with me for a very long time.

THE ONE WHO KEEPS US TOGETHER ♡

Meet our
teacher.

Renee van den Berg

Behind every great ensemble is someone who knows when to push, when to listen and when to tell us to practise that section one more time.

RENEE'S STORY

I love to play and coach Marimba because of the joy it brings the player and listener alike. There is no other instrument that brings together people like the marimba - it is simply magnetic! The marimba is also accessible to people from all ages, walks of life and abilities.
